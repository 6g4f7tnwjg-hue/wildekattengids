Snow leopard

It is minus twenty. For hours you have been staring through the scope at a rock face that looks the same everywhere. Then the spotter next to you whispers one word, and suddenly you see it: a grey cat with a tail like a scarf, stretched out on a ledge. In the Rumbak Valley of Hemis in Ladakh this happens every winter, as long as you give it a week. Chance: 5 – with patience, good spotters and warm clothes, the ghost of the mountains really can be found here.

Best places

  • Kibber (Spiti) (India) – chance 4
