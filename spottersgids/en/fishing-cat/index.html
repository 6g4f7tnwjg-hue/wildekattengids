Fishing cat

It is two in the morning and the jeep is crawling past a pond between the houses. Nobody says a word. Then the guide whispers: there, on the little wall in the water. A stocky, striped cat with a short tail looks at you for a moment, then slips into the reeds. The fishing cat lives right next to people, among paddy fields, canals and fish ponds, but seeing one takes night after night of silent searching. Chance: 4 – in Sri Lanka most dedicated searchers succeed, but sometimes only after five or six nights.
