Pallas' cat

It is five in the morning on the steppe and the light is still pink. On top of a boulder sits a flat, fluffy face with two yellow eyes. The manul doesn't move: it is counting on you to mistake it for a rock. In eastern Mongolia local spotters know where the dens are, and in July you can see one almost every day, sometimes with kittens. Elsewhere it means hours of scanning rocks and pika colonies. Chance: 5 – on a dedicated trip in Mongolia you rarely go home empty-handed.
