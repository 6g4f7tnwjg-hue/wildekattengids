Kodkod

It is almost midnight in the rainforest of Chiloé. You have been standing still on a forest track for hours, torch off, moss dripping. Then a soft thud right next to you: on a fallen log sits a jet-black cat, barely bigger than a house cat, staring at you. Ten seconds, and it melts into the ferns. The kodkod is the smallest cat in the Americas, and seeing one takes patience. Chance: 3 – with a few nights of dedicated searching on Chiloé you have a reasonable chance, but it is never certain.

Best places

  • Parque Tepuhueico (Chiloé) (Chile) – chance 3
  • Petrohué (Vicente Pérez Rosales) (Chile) – chance 2
  • Nahuel Huapi (Puerto Blest) (Argentina) – chance 1
  • Rest of Chiloé (Cucao, Chepu) (Chile) – chance 1
