Cougar

A guanaco on the ridge freezes and starts to bray, shrill and relentless. Every pair of binoculars in the car swings the same way. There, between the low shrubs, walks a puma, calmly, as if the whole steppe belongs to her. On the eastern side of Torres del Paine this has become routine: with a local tracker most visitors see several pumas in a few days. Elsewhere in the Americas, from Canada to Mexico, it remains a ghost. Chance: 5 – in Patagonia you rarely go home empty-handed.
