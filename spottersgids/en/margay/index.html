Margay

It is two in the morning in the cloud forest. Your torch catches something on a branch above the trail: huge eyes, a long spotted tail. A margay. Moments later it climbs head-first down the trunk, as if gravity did not exist, and is gone. Such a moment is rare; even seasoned cat hunters often miss it. Chance: 2 – only with patience, many night walks and luck.
