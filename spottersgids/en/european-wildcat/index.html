European wildcat

It is half past seven in the morning and the dew still lies on the freshly mown hay meadow. In the middle of the field a grey-brown cat sits perfectly still, ears forward. Then a pounce, a brief struggle, and she walks off with a water vole in her jaws. In the mountains around Riaño in northern Spain you can often watch this from your car in August and September. Elsewhere in Europe the wildcat is a forest ghost you will almost never see. Chance: 5 – in the Cantabrian mountains after mowing you will nearly always find one.
