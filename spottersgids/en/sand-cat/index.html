Sand cat

It is past midnight on the road to Aousserd. For hours you have seen nothing but gravel, sand and an endless sky full of stars. Then, low to the ground, two small green-white eyes stare back at you for a long time. A cat with a broad head and low-set ears presses itself flat against a grass tussock and does not move. You have to earn a moment like this: most groups that search here for several nights see one, but every season some people go home empty-handed. Chance: 4 – good, if you give it several nights.
