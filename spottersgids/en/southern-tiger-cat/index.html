Southern tiger cat

It has poured for a full day in the valleys north of Caxias do Sul. Then the sky breaks, and at ten in the morning a small, dark cat with a short tail crosses the forest road. A few seconds, nothing more. For the spotter in that car it was the highlight of the trip, and one of the very few documented sightings of this species by a traveller. Chance: 1 – a matter of great luck.
