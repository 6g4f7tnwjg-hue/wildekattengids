Eurasian lynx

It is three in the morning in an Estonian pine forest. A warm blob lights up on the thermal scope, at the edge of a snowy field. The spotlight comes on and there it is: ear tufts, a stubby tail, yellow eyes calmly staring back at you. Seeing a lynx anywhere in Europe is usually down to luck, but in Lahemaa in February and March a surprising number of groups succeed. Chance: 3 – with a few nights of driving and a guide who knows the area, you have a real shot.
