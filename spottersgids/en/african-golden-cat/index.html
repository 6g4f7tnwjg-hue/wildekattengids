African golden cat

Mist hangs between the giant trees of Kibale. You crawl along a red forest track, eyes on the verge. Then something reddish-brown crosses, low and sturdy, and it is gone. That is how long an encounter with an African golden cat usually lasts, if you are lucky at all. Even researchers only saw one with their own eyes after years of fieldwork. Chance: 2 – only with a lot of patience and a good dose of luck.
