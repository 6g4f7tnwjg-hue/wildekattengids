Rusty-spotted cat

It is past midnight on a dirt road along the edge of Wilpattu. The lamp catches a pair of eyes in a bush, barely half a metre off the ground. A kitten-sized cat, smaller than your own house cat, with rusty-brown spots, stares at you for a moment and then shoots up a tree. The rusty-spotted cat is the smallest cat in the world. In Sri Lanka's dry north, most dedicated night searchers find one within a few nights. Chance: 4 – with a good local guide and a few late night drives, it often works out.
