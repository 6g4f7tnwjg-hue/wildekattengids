Jaguar

The boat's engine cuts out. On the bank, half hidden among the water hyacinths, a jaguar lies looking straight at you. In the Pantanal near Porto Jofre that is not a dream but just another Tuesday morning. Between July and October the cats hunt along the rivers, and most visitors see several within three days. Chance: 5 – you rarely go home empty-handed here.
