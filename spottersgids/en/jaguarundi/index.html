Jaguarundi

It is nine in the morning and the sun is already high above the fields and forest edges. A dark, elongated animal slips across the dirt road in front of you. It looks more like an otter than a cat: small head, short legs, long tail. Two seconds later the jaguarundi has vanished into the scrub. This cat hunts by day, yet few people ever get a good look at one. Chance: 3 – with patience and plenty of kilometres on the dirt tracks, it is doable.
