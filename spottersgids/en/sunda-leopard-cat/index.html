Sunda leopard cat

The jeep bumps along the forest road at Tabin, rainforest on one side, rows of oil palms on the other. After the afternoon rain the frog chorus is deafening. Then a cat stands on the verge, spotted like a miniature leopard, a mouse in its jaws. A little later another, and another. On Borneo the Sunda leopard cat is the cat you actually do see: one group counted nine in a single night. Chance: 5 – a few night drives in Sabah will almost certainly get you one.
