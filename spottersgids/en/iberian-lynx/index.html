Iberian lynx

It is January, half past seven in the morning, and frost still clings to the granite boulders. Along the viewpoint, dozens of telescopes point at the same slope. Then someone whispers: 'There!' Between the holm oaks walks an Iberian lynx, ear tufts up, spotted coat lit by the first sun. In the Sierra de Andújar this is no fairy tale any more: during the mating season most well-prepared visitors see at least one within a few days. Chance: 5 – in winter, with patience, you rarely go home empty-handed here.
