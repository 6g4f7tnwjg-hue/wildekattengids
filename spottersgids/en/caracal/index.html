Caracal

It is a quarter to seven and the gate is about to close. Then a flock of helmeted guineafowl starts to scold. A hundred metres further on it stands there: russet, slender, with long black ear tufts. For a moment it looks at you, then it melts into the bush. That is how it usually goes with the caracal: days of nothing, then two minutes you will never forget. Chance: 2 – even in the best areas you need patience and luck.
