Serval

Far out in the yellow grass, two huge ears stand upright. Then a slender, spotted cat springs a metre into the air and lands front paws first, right on a mouse. That is how a serval hunts: by ear, with leaps like a coiled spring. On the floor of Ngorongoro Crater and in the eastern Serengeti you can watch this show with some patience and a guide who scans well. Chance: 4 – search deliberately and your odds are good.
