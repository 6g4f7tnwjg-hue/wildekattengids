Leopard cat

It is dark in the forested valleys of Tangjiahe. The spotlight slides along the verge, past a group of takin, and then two green eyes catch the beam. A cat the size of a house cat, black spots on a golden-brown coat, looks into the light for a moment and then slips into the undergrowth. In this Sichuan reserve recent visitors usually found the leopard cat within a night or two. Chance: 4 – give it a few nights of spotlighting and your odds here are good.
