Bay cat

This is the holy grail of cat-watching. Deep in the rainforest of Borneo lives a cat that almost nobody has ever seen with their own eyes. Even researchers who run camera traps there for years often know it only from a handful of grainy pictures. Going anyway? Then do it for the adventure, and if you do see one, you join a very small club. Chance: 1 – only with a great deal of luck.
