African wildcat

The sun has just cleared the red dunes of the Kalahari. In the dry bed of the Auob River, a slender, sand-coloured cat slips through the grass. Long legs, a ringed tail, and when it glances back you catch the rusty-red backs of its ears. This is the wild ancestor of every house cat on Earth, and here it is truly wild. In Kgalagadi visitors often see several in a single morning. Chance: 5 – in the Kalahari you will almost always see one.
