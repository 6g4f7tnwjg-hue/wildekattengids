    Jungle cat

    The sun still hangs low over the grassland of Velavadar. Between the blackbuck a slender, sand-coloured cat slinks past on long legs, with a short, ringed tail. It glances back once, then strolls on into the tall grass. In the cool months jungle cats are out and about here in broad daylight, and some visitors see several in a single morning. Chance: 5 – in western India this is about the easiest wild cat there is.
