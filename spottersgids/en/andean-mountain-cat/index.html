Andean mountain cat

It is past midnight at 4,000 metres and your breath freezes in the torch beam. For hours you have been scanning rock walls where viscachas live, without seeing a thing. Then two eyes keep glowing on a ledge. Grey, thick rings around a long tail: an Andean mountain cat, one of the rarest cats in the Americas. Only in the Chilean altiplano around Lauca do visitors see it now and then, and often only after a week of searching. Chance: 2 – with many nights and an experienced guide you have a small chance.
