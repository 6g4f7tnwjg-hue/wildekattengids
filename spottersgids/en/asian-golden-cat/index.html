Asian golden cat

It is the middle of the day in the mountain forests of Arunachal Pradesh. The car crawls along the narrow forest track, mist hanging between the oaks. Then a red-brown cat steps out of the undergrowth, a metre in front of you, and two seconds later it is gone. That is how it goes with the Asian golden cat: you can search for twelve nights and see nothing, or bump into one at the first try. Chance: 2 – a small chance, and only for those who bring patience.
