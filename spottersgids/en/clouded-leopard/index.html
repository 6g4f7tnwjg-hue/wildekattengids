Clouded leopard

A troop of pig-tailed macaques is scolding high in the rainforest canopy. Then you see why: stretched out on a branch lies a cat with cloud-shaped blotches and a tail almost as long as its body. That really happened in Khao Yai, but it was decades ago and has stayed rare. On mainland Asia there is still not a single place where you see the clouded leopard with any regularity. Chance: 1 – searching is an adventure, seeing one is a miracle.
