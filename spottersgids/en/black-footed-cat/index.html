Black-footed cat

It is past ten and the wind cuts across the plain. The spotlight glides slowly over short grass, past termite mounds and springhare burrows. Then two tiny lights, just above the ground, and they are gone again. The black-footed cat is Africa's smallest cat and one of the deadliest hunters on earth, but finding one is a gamble every single night: even in the best areas around Kimberley, groups drive for three nights without a sighting. Chance: 3 – with patience, dry weather and short grass it can happen, but there are no guarantees.

Best places

  • Northern Cape near Kimberley (South Africa) – chance 3
  • Upper Karoo near Hanover (South Africa) – chance 2
  • Southwestern Free State (South Africa) – chance 1
  • Southern Namibia near Grünau (Namibia) – chance 1
  • Kgalagadi Transfrontier Park (South Africa / Botswana) – chance 1
