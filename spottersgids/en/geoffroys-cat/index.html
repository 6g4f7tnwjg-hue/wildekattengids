    Geoffroy's cat

    It is pitch dark in the Chaco. The spotlight sweeps over the grass beside the dirt road and stops on two green eyes. A spotted cat sits up on its haunches like a meerkat and stares straight back at you. Then it slips away into the thorn scrub. In Paraguay and north-eastern Argentina Geoffroy's cat is no myth, but you do need to put in the night drives. Chance: 4 – with a few nights in the Chaco your odds are good.
