Marbled cat

It is two in the morning on a forestry road in Sabah. Your beam slides along the trunks and stops on two orange eyes, high up in a tree. A cat with a tail as long as its body, covered in dark blotches like marble, gazes calmly down at you. This is the marbled cat, one of the least-seen cats in the world. In Deramakot, a week of night drives gives you a fair chance. Chance: 3 – with enough nights you stand a real chance.
