Cheetah

It is seven in the morning on the plains of Ndutu. A group of Thomson's gazelles stands frozen, all staring the same way. Then you see it: a slender, spotted cat on a termite mound, eyes fixed on the herd. A few minutes later the sprint explodes, faster than your camera can follow. Between December and March this is no rarity in the southern Serengeti. Chance: 5 – in calving season you almost always see them.
