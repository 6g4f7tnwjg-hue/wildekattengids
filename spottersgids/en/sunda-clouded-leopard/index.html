Sunda clouded leopard

It is past ten on a muddy forest road in Deramakot. The spotlight slides along the verge and stops on two eyes. Then she steps onto the road: a female Sunda clouded leopard, who sits down a few metres from the vehicle and yawns. You never forget a quarter of an hour like that. But many searchers drive here every night for a week without seeing one. Chance: 3 – with a full week of night drives you have a reasonable chance, no more.
