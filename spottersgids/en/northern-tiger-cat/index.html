Northern tiger cat

It is night in the caatinga. Thorn scrub rustles in the warm wind, and somewhere among the cacti slinks a cat the size of a house cat, sprinkled with small rosettes. Tomorrow a researcher will find it on a camera trap; you, tonight, almost certainly will not. The northern tiger cat is one of South America's least-seen cats, and genuine sightings by travellers are rare. Chance: 1 – only with a great deal of luck, or by camera trap.

Best places

  • Mirador State Park (Brazil) – chance 1
  • Furna Feia and the caatinga of Rio Grande do Norte (Brazil) – chance 1
  • Rupununi savannas (Guyana) – chance 1
