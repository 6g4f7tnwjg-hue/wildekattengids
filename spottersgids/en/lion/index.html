Lion

It is still dark in the Mara when the sound rolls through your tent: a deep, swelling roar that carries for miles. At first light you find them, three males flat in the grass, bellies round from a kill. In the Masai Mara, the Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater this is entirely normal. For the rare Asiatic lion you need to go to Gir in India, and for Africa's northern lion to Zakouma in Chad. Chance: 5 – in East Africa you will almost certainly see them.
