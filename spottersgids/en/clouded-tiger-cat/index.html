    Clouded tiger cat

    It is ten at night on a narrow mountain road in the cloud forest above Quito. Your torch slides along mossy trunks and dripping ferns. Then a shrill alarm call rings out: an olinguito in a tree is scolding something on the road. In the beam stands a cat barely bigger than a house cat, covered in small rosettes. A second later the clouded tiger cat has vanished back into the mist. Chance: 3 – with a few evenings of spotlighting you have a reasonable chance.
