Tilcayo

Mist hangs between the tree ferns and every leaf is dripping. Somewhere in this cloud forest on the eastern flank of the Bolivian Andes slinks a spotted cat of one and a half kilos, smaller than a house cat. Only in September 2026 did it get its own name: Leopardus tilcayo, the first new cat species in more than a hundred years. So far science knows exactly two live animals and a handful of camera-trap images. You don't come here to see it, but to walk in its world. Chance: 1 – almost nobody has ever seen one in the wild.
